Product Concept

Start dinner with what is already at home.

UseFirst prioritizes the nearest date so you cook what matters first. Rather than requiring users to scan dozens of barcodes or audit full pantries, UseFirst tests a lightweight habit: entering 3–5 perishables and receiving an immediate sorted urgency list and simple recipe ideas.
